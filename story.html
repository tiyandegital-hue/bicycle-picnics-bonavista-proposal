Our story

A women-owned café,
at the edge of
the Atlantic.

Bicycle Picnics Café & Bistro sits oceanfront in Bonavista, Newfoundland — serving fresh sit-in and take-out meals for breakfast, lunch and supper, with daily cookies, cakes and biscuits from the oven.

Why the bicycles?

Bonavista is made for slow days — cliffs, lighthouses, salt air. The idea is simple: give people a bicycle, a basket full of good food, and the whole coastline. No itinerary, no hurry — just ride out and picnic wherever the view wins.

Back at the café, it’s coffee, tea, espresso and soda, vegetarian-friendly and vegan options, and a warm welcome for everyone who walks in.
