Reserve a Picnic

The signature experience

Rent the bike.
Pack the basket.
Ride the coast.

Our picnic rentals come with a charming basket filled with meals, desserts and drinks — with a picnic blanket tucked in. Ride out and picnic wherever you choose.

  1. 01

    Pick up your ride

    Start at the café at 34 Mockbeggar Rd. Choose your bicycle, tell us who’s eating, and we fill your basket to order.

  2. 02

    Ride the coast

    Pedal the scenic Bonavista shoreline — guests ride out toward the lighthouse with the basket strapped securely behind.

  3. 03

    Spread the blanket

    Find your spot, spread the blanket, and picnic on the edge of the North Atlantic. SAMPLE Route details confirmed with the café.
