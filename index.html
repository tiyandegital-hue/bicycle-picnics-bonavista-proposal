The signature

The ride-and-picnic.

Pick up a bicycle at the café, a wicker basket strapped on behind — filled with your meals, desserts and drinks, with a picnic blanket tucked in. Then pedal the coast and stop wherever the view wins.

Breakfast, lunch and supper are served fresh in the café too — sit-in or take-out — with coffee, tea, espresso and soda, plus daily cookies, cakes and biscuits.
